Over-explaining

One pattern, read all the way through.

trying to make them understand

Move toward Communication / Conflict
What it does: The other person floods and withdraws more. The explaining confirms the pursuer dynamic.
A way through: one clear, short statement of need. Then hold the silence.
Why this made sense: You were trying to secure understanding and prevent abandonment through words and clarity.
What keeps it in place: If you stop, you may have to accept that they simply are not meeting you.
Your own anger and grief about the gap between what you need and what you’re getting.
What stopping feels like: Without the right explanation, you will be misunderstood, blamed, or left.

This is a defense you built for a reason. Not a flaw in you.

Feel your feet, or the chair, before you read on.

What if it comes true

You say it once, you stop explaining, and they still don’t get it. The gap is real and it stays. They don’t change their mind, and you didn’t earn the understanding you were working for.

What holds: inside you

You’ve already lived through being misunderstood and kept going. You’re not actually managed by it. Knowing the truth, even an unwanted one, is something you’ve carried before and can carry again.

What holds: with another person

When you’re ready: one person who lets the gap be real without rushing to fix it shows you that being incompletely understood isn’t the same as being left.

One move

Say the thing once. Then stop, and let the silence sit for one breath without adding to it.

You can sit here. There’s no next thing required.
