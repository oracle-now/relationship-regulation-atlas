Why do we do what we do—and what does it cost us?

The Relationship Regulation Atlas maps the strategies people use to manage overwhelming emotion, closeness, and conflict. Every behavior here is an attempt to survive something. This atlas names the logic, the cost, and the way through.

How to use this atlas

1. Find yourself. Browse the matrix and notice which behaviors feel familiar. Click any row to expand the full picture.
2. Map your cycle. Use the Pairing Map to see what happens when your move meets your partner's move.
3. Read the resistance. Each behavior includes what makes it hard to change—and what it's really protecting.
Built from: polyvagal theory, EFT, IFS, attachment research, and the clinical frameworks of Orna Guralnik, the Gottmans, Van der Kolk, Schwartz, and Bradshaw.

Three ways to use this

For yourself

Browse the matrix and notice which behaviors feel uncomfortably familiar. The resistance column is where the real work starts.

With a partner

Use the Cycle Map together. Each person picks their move. The loop it shows you is not an accusation—it's a description of something you're both caught in.

In therapy

The atlas was built with the clinical frameworks of Orna Guralnik, the Gottmans, Van der Kolk, Dick Schwartz, and John Bradshaw. It is designed to complement, not replace, therapeutic work.
