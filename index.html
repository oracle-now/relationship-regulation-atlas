Pursuer / protest behavior
Move toward Discharge Romantic partnership
The logic: If I push hard enough, they will finally see me and respond.
The cost: The other person floods and pulls away further. The pursuit becomes self-fulfilling.
Resistance: If I stop pursuing, I’m accepting that I don’t matter enough to reach for.
What it protects: The grief of already knowing the answer.
A way through: Speak the soft emotion underneath the protest. “I’m scared. I miss you. I feel invisible.”
