# Role intelligence boundary

This public repository subscribes to the pinned private role-intelligence snapshot `834bf1357485b17872a0f835308f7e95cb727590`. The subscription identifies role names and policy boundaries only; it contains no private source material or credentials.

## Review levels

Ordinary navigation, layout, and reversible interface changes are Level 1 and require a maker plus one counterweight. Behavioral interpretation and emotionally consequential content are Level 2 and require an independent challenger, evidence steward, and human decider. Diagnosis, crisis, treatment, or clinical-outcome implications are Level 3 and require qualified human review.

## Counterbalances

- Behavioral interpretations are challenged by UX research and mental-health review.
- Mental-health framing is challenged by behavioral science, content design, and product judgment.
- Product design is challenged by research and accessibility review.
- Simplification cannot remove uncertainty, safety boundaries, or referral guidance.

## Public-repository boundary

Do not place private registry contents, private research briefs, credentials, personal data, participant records, or licensed source text in this repository. Any generated public artifact must contain only approved summaries and public source links.

## Prohibited automation

Role intelligence cannot diagnose a visitor, approve treatment guidance, alter production code, publish changes, or replace qualified professional review.
