Lineage · Influences · Gratitude

Standing on their shoulders

This atlas grew out of the people whose work helped me put language around things I was already noticing. Some helped me understand the body. Some helped me think more clearly about attachment. Some helped me hold desire, shame, fear, and protection in the same frame without flattening any of it.

I did not include these voices just because they are respected. I included them because I actually followed them over time — read them, watched them, learned from them, and found their ideas quietly shaping how I think about relationships.

These are not just references to me. They are part of the emotional and intellectual lineage of the atlas. Some gave me backbone. Some gave me texture. Some helped me hold people with more complexity and more care.

Relationship Regulation Atlas — a work in progress. Built with care.
