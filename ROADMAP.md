# Roadmap

This is a living document. It holds the three eras and the product ladder as the room understood them on 2026-05-29.

None of this is a promise. It is a direction. The direction can change when the work changes it.

---

## The founder's constraints (non-negotiable)

- No recurring people-time. No cohorts, no DMs, no Zooms, no community moderation.
- Work up front, then mostly hands-off until it really takes off.
- Right now the goal is visibility in the lineage audience: therapists, coaches, psychologically curious people who speak this language.
- The mechanism and the mapping are the cool thing. Not a service wrapped around it.
- Entry-level offer (no credential, no platform) is different from the mature ones.

---

## Three eras

### Era 1: Now

No product. No offer. No pitch.

The atlas grows publicly. Social extracts one pattern per week. The URL lives in bio. The lineage audience finds it or they don't. Revenue: zero. Cannot be rushed and should not be monetized yet.

The job in this era: keep building. The mapping is the content. Social posts are extracts of the mechanism, not marketing for a product.

**Social format:** One pattern. Three paragraphs: what the behavior looks like, the logic underneath it, the cost. No jargon. No CTA. End with: "the full map lives here" and the URL. That's the post.

### Era 2: First products

Triggered when three or more contexts are substantially complete.

**Product 1: Weekly practice sequence**
- 52 entries. One per week: pattern, reflection prompt, somatic cue, one move.
- Written once, loaded into a drip (Beehiiv or a member page), runs forever.
- Founder time after launch: near zero.
- Low price, recurring.
- Purpose: fund the writing time for everything else.
- Build time estimate: 3 to 4 months.
- Price range: $8 to $15 per month.

**Product 2: Extended atlas**
- All contexts complete. Lineage annotations. Self-assessment that maps your cluster. Downloadable pattern reference.
- One-time purchase. The free atlas remains free forever. This is the deep version.
- The Stripe Press model: the free thing proves it is worth buying the paid thing.
- Build time: one substantial writing project after the atlas is complete.
- Price range: $40 to $80 one-time.

### Era 3: If it takes off

**Product 3: Practitioner tool**
- Atlas reformatted as a clinical resource.
- Pattern entries as psychoeducation handouts.
- Five-part structure as assessment guide.
- Lineage block annotated for clinical context.
- Sold to therapists and coaches who buy tools and use them for years.
- Separate practitioner-facing page, different register from the public atlas.
- Highest ceiling of all four products.
- Build trigger: extended atlas has been on the market for at least six months with demonstrated practitioner interest.
- Price range: $150 to $350.

**Physical atlas (only if it really succeeds)**
- Printed. Designed. Limited edition.
- Pre-order model: sell to the mailing list before going to print.
- Year two at the earliest.
- Price range: $45 to $90.
- Build trigger: mailing list large enough to pre-sell the print run.

## The Gwern path

Stays open underneath all of this. The free atlas keeps growing. Authority compounds. The products fund the time to keep building. If the consulting or hired-for-thinking moment comes, it opens by itself. It cannot be forced.

The conditions the Gwern model requires:
- Depth over breadth, indefinitely
- The free work has to become the thing people in the field cite
- Original framework: the five-part structure is not available elsewhere at this depth in this register
- Plain language for clinical insight: rare
- Consistent publishing over years, not months

The atlas already meets these conditions. The question is only whether the work continues.

---

## What is not on the roadmap

These are explicitly removed until conditions change:

- 1:1 sessions
- Cohorts or group programs
- Community requiring moderation
- Practitioner training (revisit only if practitioner tool has 12+ months of market signal)
- Physical atlas before Era 3 is confirmed

---

## Reference entities

The register and aesthetic the atlas aspires to:
- Are.na: the aesthetic IS the signal. Slow, associative, non-commercial.
- Stripe Press: the design communicates that the maker thinks carefully.
- Gwern: free, forever. The paid thing (consulting) follows from authority, not from a funnel.
