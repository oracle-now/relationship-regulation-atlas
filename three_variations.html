Three layout variations

Over-explaining

Three ways to present this pattern. All three use the same content. The differences are structural.

Variation A — Standard card (current branch format)

Full five-part structure, always expanded. Best for first-time reading.

Over-explaining — trying to make them understand
Move toward Communication / Conflict
The logic: Tries to secure understanding and prevent abandonment through words and clarity.
The cost: The other person floods and withdraws more; the explaining confirms the pursuer dynamic.
Resistance: If I stop, I may have to accept that they simply are not meeting me.
What it protects: My own anger and grief about the gap between what I need and what I’m getting.
A way through: One clear, short statement of need. Then hold the silence.

Variation C — Resourcing layer integrated

The full resourcing sequence from the atlas detail panel, shown inline within the card. The deepest read. Best for a dedicated single-pattern page.

Over-explaining — trying to make them understand
Move toward Communication / Conflict
The logic: Tries to secure understanding and prevent abandonment through words and clarity.
The cost: The other person floods and withdraws more; the explaining confirms the pursuer dynamic.
Resistance: If I stop, I may have to accept that they simply are not meeting me.
What it protects: My own anger and grief about the gap between what I need and what I’m getting.
What I fear if I stop: Without the right explanation, I will be misunderstood, blamed, or left.
A way through: One clear, short statement of need. Then hold the silence.
Feel your feet, or the chair, before you read on.

This is a defense you built for a reason. Not a flaw in you.

What if it comes true

You say it once, you stop explaining, and they still don’t get it. The gap is real and it stays.

What holds: inside you

You’ve already lived through being misunderstood and kept going. You’re not actually managed by it.

One move

Say the thing once. Then stop, and let the silence sit for one breath without adding to it.
